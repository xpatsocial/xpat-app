# SVP Verification Report: Market & Product Intelligence Claims

**Prepared by:** SVP Research & Intelligence, Aych Holdings LLC
**Date:** April 8, 2026
**Status:** INDEPENDENT VERIFICATION of VP Market Intelligence and VP Product Intelligence reports

---

## Methodology

Every major data point from three reports was cross-referenced against primary sources, affiliate program pages, company announcements, earnings calls, and third-party databases. Where claims could not be verified from a reliable primary source, they are flagged. Where claims are wrong, corrected figures are provided.

---

## 1. VERIFIED CLAIMS (with primary sources)

### 1.1 SafetyWing 10% Recurring Commission
- **Claim:** SafetyWing offers 10% monthly recurring commission with a 365-day cookie.
- **Verdict:** VERIFIED
- **Source:** SafetyWing's own Ambassador Program page (safetywing.com/ambassador) and confirmed by multiple affiliate directories including [GetLasso](https://getlasso.co/affiliate/safetywing/) and [Post Affiliate Pro](https://www.postaffiliatepro.com/affiliate-program-directory/safetywing-affiliate-program/).
- **Detail:** 10% commission on all renewals within 364 days of the initial click. No minimum payout threshold. Payments via PayPal, bank, or check.

### 1.2 Wise Commission Rates (GBP 10 personal, GBP 50 business)
- **Claim:** Wise pays $10-50 per signup.
- **Verdict:** VERIFIED (with currency clarification)
- **Source:** [Wise Affiliate Program page](https://wise.com/gb/affiliate-program/) and [Dollarbreak 2026 guide](https://dollarbreak.co.ke/wise-affiliate-program/).
- **Correction:** The rates are GBP 10 (personal) and GBP 50 (business), not USD. At current exchange rates (~1.27 USD/GBP), that is approximately $12.70 and $63.50. The report's "$10-50" range is roughly correct but understates the business tier. Also, commissions are CPA on funded accounts (completed cross-currency transaction), not just signups. Wise offers lifetime attribution (no cookie window) -- this was correctly reported.

### 1.3 NordVPN 100% First Month + 30% Recurring
- **Claim:** NordVPN pays 100% of the first month plus recurring commissions.
- **Verdict:** VERIFIED
- **Source:** [NordVPN Affiliate page](https://nordvpn.com/affiliate/) and [multiple](https://afprogs.com/nordvpn-affiliate-program/) [2026 reviews](https://commissiondex.com/programs/nordvpn/).
- **Detail:** 100% first month applies to 1-month plans. For 1-year and 2-year plans, it is 40% on new signups (not 100%). Recurring is 30% on all renewals. 30-day cookie. $100 minimum payout.
- **Nuance the report missed:** The "100% first month" headline is accurate only for monthly plans. Most users buy annual/biennial plans where the commission is 40%, not 100%. The report's claim of "up to $57 per 2-year plan signup" is plausible given 40% of a ~$140 2-year plan.

### 1.4 Hostelworld Social Pass Pricing and Launch Date
- **Claim:** Social Pass launched November 2025, priced EUR 4.99/week to EUR 59.99/year.
- **Verdict:** VERIFIED
- **Source:** [Hostelworld blog announcement](https://www.hostelworld.com/blog/hostelworld-social-pass-the-social-app-no-booking-required/) (November 13, 2025) and [Hostelworld H2 2025 earnings call](https://www.investing.com/news/transcripts/earnings-call-transcript-hostelworld-sees-growth-in-h2-2025-q1-2026-93CH-4581868).
- **Additional data:** 37% of Social Pass buyers are new to Hostelworld. Early mix is ~2/3 weekly, ~1/3 monthly. The report's pricing tiers (EUR 4.99/week, EUR 9.99/month, EUR 19.99/3 months, EUR 59.99/year) are confirmed.

### 1.5 InterNations 5M+ Members, 420 Cities, 6,000+ Events/Month
- **Claim:** InterNations has 5M members across 420 cities with 6,000+ monthly events.
- **Verdict:** VERIFIED (slightly outdated)
- **Source:** [InterNations Factsheet 2023-2024](https://cms.in-cdn.net/cdn/file/cms-media/public/2023-12/InterNations-Factsheet_2023-12.pdf) and [InterNations LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/company/internations).
- **Update:** As of late 2024, the figure is closer to 5.2-5.5 million members. The 420 cities and 6,000+ events/month figures remain consistent across sources.

### 1.6 Couchsurfing Paywall Date and Community Impact
- **Claim:** Paywall introduced May 14, 2020; locked users out of profiles, messages, connections.
- **Verdict:** VERIFIED
- **Source:** [Bren on The Road](https://brenontheroad.com/the-end-of-couchsurfing/), [Couchers.org](https://couchers.org/issues/profit-and-incentives), [Couchsurfing Blog](https://blog.couchsurfing.com/we-hear-you/).
- **Detail:** The paywall went live May 14, 2020. The founder's 2011 promise that the platform would "never make you pay" is documented. Alternatives like Couchers.org, BeWelcome, and Trustroots emerged directly in response. Trustroots reported ~10,000 new members in 2020 attributed to the paywall exodus.

### 1.7 Digital Nomad Population 43-45M
- **Claim:** 43-45 million global digital nomads growing at 21% CAGR.
- **Verdict:** PARTIALLY VERIFIED
- **Source:** [Nomads.com 2026 statistics](https://nomads.com/digital-nomad-statistics), [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/digital-nomads-statistics/), [Atlys](https://www.atlys.com/blog/digital-nomad-statistics).
- **Detail:** The 40-45M range is the consensus across multiple 2025-2026 sources. The 21% CAGR claim was NOT independently verified -- most sources cite growth trends but do not specify a single CAGR figure. The US alone has ~18.1-18.5M digital nomads (MBO Partners data). The total number is directionally correct but inherently imprecise given no universal definition of "digital nomad."

## 2. CORRECTED CLAIMS (original vs actual)

### 2.1 Nomad List Revenue: $5.3M is CUMULATIVE, Not Annual
- **Original claim (competitive report):** "Revenue: $5.3M in 2024 (up from $704K in 2023 -- 7.5x growth)"
- **Original claim (monetization report):** "$5.3M cumulative, 29K customers, ~$440K ARR recently"
- **Actual:** The two reports CONTRADICT each other. The competitive report says $5.3M is 2024 annual revenue. The monetization report says it is cumulative.
- **What GetLatka actually says:** "How Nomad List hit $5.3M revenue and 29K customers in 2024" -- the phrasing "hit $5.3M" combined with the historical revenue trail ($120K in 2017, $310K in 2020, $505K in 2021, $700K in 2022, $704K in 2023) strongly suggests this is CUMULATIVE LIFETIME REVENUE. If you add those years up: ~$120K + ~$200K + ~$310K + ~$505K + ~$700K + ~$704K + remaining years = roughly $3-4M through 2023. A jump to $5.3M cumulative by late 2024 is plausible with ~$1-1.5M added in 2024.
- **Supporting evidence:** Multiple sources describe Pieter Levels as making "$3M/year" across ALL his businesses (Nomad List + RemoteOK + PhotoAI etc.), not $5.3M from Nomad List alone. A $5.3M single-year from Nomad List alone would be inconsistent with the ~$3M total figure widely cited.
- **Corrected figure:** $5.3M is almost certainly CUMULATIVE lifetime revenue, not 2024 annual. Annual Nomad List revenue is likely in the $700K-$1.5M range. ARR is approximately $440K based on the monetization report's own figure.
- **Impact:** The competitive report's framing of "7.5x growth" from $704K to $5.3M is MISLEADING. There was no 7.5x annual growth. The actual year-over-year growth is more modest.
- **Sources:** [GetLatka](https://getlatka.com/companies/nomad-list), [Starter Story](https://www.starterstory.com/stories/nomad-list-breakdown), [Software Growth](https://www.softwaregrowth.io/blog/how-pieter-levels-grew-nomad-list)

### 2.2 Nomad List Pricing: $99 is Outdated -- Current Price is ~$300
- **Original claim:** "$99 one-time lifetime membership (historical: $200-$300, frequent 50% discounts)"
- **Actual:** Nomad List (now rebranded to Nomads.com) currently charges $299.98 for lifetime access. The $99 figure is an older or discounted price point, not the current standard price.
- **Source:** [Nomads.com](https://nomads.com/), [PilotPlans review (Nov 2025)](https://www.pilotplans.com/blog/nomad-list-review), [SmartBranding](https://smartbranding.com/nomad-list-rebrands-to-nomads-com-representing-millions-of-digital-nomads-worldwide/).
- **Impact:** The competitive report's pricing comparison table should list $299.98, not $99-$300. The current price is at the top of the stated range, not the bottom. This actually STRENGTHENS x/pat's free positioning -- the gap is even wider than presented.

### 2.3 Airalo Commission: 10%, NOT 15%
- **Original claim:** "Airalo: 15% per qualifying sale"
- **Actual:** Airalo's affiliate program offers approximately 10% commission, not 15%.
- **Source:** [Airalo Partners page](https://partners.airalo.com/solutions/affiliates), [Affiliate.watch](https://affiliate.watch/affiliate/airalo), [Cuelinks listing at 8.55%](https://www.cuelinks.com/campaigns/airalo-affiliate-program).
- **Impact on revenue projections:** The monetization report estimates $1.50-$3.75 per eSIM transaction at 15%. At the actual 10% rate, this drops to $1.00-$2.50 per transaction. Annual eSIM revenue per user drops from $6-22 to $4-15. This is a ~33% reduction in the eSIM affiliate revenue line.

### 2.4 Meetup Organizer Pricing: NOT $55/month -- Actual Range is $16.49-$48/month
- **Original claim:** "Organizer pricing: $55/month per group (Meetup Pro)" and "organizer costs have increased 4x over time."
- **Actual:** Current pricing (2026) ranges from $16.49/month (Basic organizer, single group) to approximately $47-48/month for higher-tier plans. Meetup Pro pricing for enterprise/multi-group is separate and higher.
- **"4x increase" claim:** Not precisely verified. What IS documented: annual plans went from ~$109 to ~$215 in 2024 (roughly 2x in one year), and Meetup Pro was tripled with less than 30 days' notice. The cumulative effect over multiple years could approach 3-4x, but "4x" is not sourced to a specific data point.
- **Source:** [Meetup Help Center](https://help.meetup.com/hc/en-us/articles/28677808413197-Organizer-Subscription-prices-overview), [Meetup Blog](https://www.meetup.com/blog/new-organizer-pricing-key-improvements/), [Hacker News discussion](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=40854275).
- **Impact:** The competitive narrative about Meetup pricing backlash is directionally correct and well-documented. The specific $55 figure is inaccurate for the standard organizer plan.

### 2.5 Polarsteps: Now 20M Users, Not 18M
- **Original claim:** "18M+ users"
- **Actual:** Polarsteps had 18M users as of December 2025 but has since reached approximately 20M users as of early 2026.
- **Source:** [Polarsteps News (Dec 2025)](https://news.polarsteps.com/news/polarsteps-unpacked-and-2025-travel-report-the-year-in-travel), [Tracxn](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/polarsteps/__Q-i1_EyIFQ7H8ztDGL5LpHMOoZvC18hrwpY-xIskvKg).
- **Impact:** Minor -- directionally correct, just slightly outdated. The growth from 18M to 20M in a few months actually strengthens the report's argument about Polarsteps' product-led growth power.

### 2.6 Couchsurfing Verification Fee: Variable, Not a Fixed $58
- **Original claim:** "$58 EUR verification fee"
- **Actual:** The verification fee varies by region and has been reported at anywhere from $56 EUR to $70 EUR. Couchsurfing's own support page does not publish a single fixed price for verification. Multiple Trustpilot reviews cite being charged amounts in the EUR 56-70 range without clear upfront disclosure.
- **Source:** [Couchsurfing FAQ](https://support.couchsurfing.org/hc/en-us/articles/214633027-Verification-Payment-Questions), [Trustpilot reviews](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/www.couchsurfing.com).
- **Impact:** Minor. The core narrative about hidden fees is correct and well-documented. The exact amount varies by user.

## 3. UNVERIFIABLE CLAIMS (no reliable primary source found)

### 3.1 "Couchsurfing 8% active accounts, 42% signup drop after paywall"
- **Original claim:** Referenced in task brief (not found in the reports themselves after review, but worth addressing).
- **Verdict:** UNVERIFIABLE. No primary source found for either the "8% active accounts" or "42% signup drop" figures.
- **What IS verifiable:** Community engagement dropped ~90% during COVID (per Couchers.org), but this conflates pandemic impact with paywall impact. Less than 4% of active members had ever financially contributed before the paywall. Trustroots gained ~10,000 new members attributed to the paywall exodus.
- **Recommendation:** Do NOT cite specific percentages for Couchsurfing's user loss without a primary source. The qualitative story (mass exodus, community betrayal, spawned alternatives) is well-documented and sufficient.

### 3.2 "Social features boost engagement 35%, retention 40%, organic growth 30%"
- **Original claim (feature report):** "Social features collectively increase engagement by 35%, retention by 40%, and organic growth by 30%" -- attributed to Social+ (social.plus).
- **Verdict:** UNVERIFIABLE as independent research. The source is a [blog post by Social+](https://www.social.plus/blog/why-social-features-are-crucial-for-in-app-user-engagement) (formerly Amity), a company that SELLS social feature SDKs. These statistics appear to be marketing claims from a vendor with a direct financial interest in promoting social features, not from independent academic research or a peer-reviewed study.
- **What IS verifiable from independent sources:** Dealer-FX saw 90% increase in retention after adding chat (cited by Stream.io). In-app community features can increase retention by 2.7x (Social+ own claim, again vendor data). 70% of apps doubled engagement after integrating chat in 2022 (Social+ statistic).
- **Recommendation:** Do NOT cite the "35%/40%/30%" figures as established research. They are vendor marketing data. Instead, cite specific case studies (Dealer-FX) or the general principle that messaging/chat features drive the highest retention among all feature types (this IS well-supported by a16z and independent research).

### 3.3 "Session interval increase predicts churn 3-7 days early"
- **Original claim (feature report):** Session interval increase is an "Early" warning signal with a "3-7 days before churn" detection window.
- **Verdict:** PARTIALLY VERIFIABLE as a general principle; the specific "3-7 days" timeframe is not sourced.
- **What IS verifiable:** Multiple sources (Braze, Iterable, Amplitude, RevenueCat) confirm that declining session frequency is a leading churn indicator. However, the specific "3-7 day" window is a reasonable heuristic presented as if it were research-backed. The actual detection window varies significantly by app category, user cohort, and engagement patterns.
- **Source:** [Braze](https://www.braze.com/resources/articles/churn-prediction), [Amplitude](https://amplitude.com/explore/analytics/churn-prediction).
- **Recommendation:** Present as "typically days to weeks before churn" rather than a precise "3-7 days" figure unless internal data confirms it post-launch.

### 3.4 Nomad List "29K paying members" vs "29K customers"
- **Original claim:** "29,000+ paying members; 10,000+ in community chat"
- **Verdict:** PARTIALLY VERIFIABLE. GetLatka lists 29,048 "customers" but does not specify whether these are currently active paying members or total lifetime customers (including churned or one-time purchasers on a lifetime plan). Given the lifetime membership model, 29K likely means total customers who have ever paid, not 29K currently active users.
- **Impact:** The distinction matters less for x/pat's competitive positioning but matters for market sizing.

## 5. REVENUE PROJECTION ASSESSMENT

### Are the assumptions reasonable?

| Assumption | Report Value | Verdict | Notes |
|-----------|-------------|---------|-------|
| Affiliate link CTR | 3% | REASONABLE | Industry average for in-app is 1-5%; 3% is mid-range |
| Affiliate conversion rate | 1.5% of clicks | REASONABLE | Travel affiliate benchmarks are 0.5-4%; 1.5% is conservative |
| Users seeing affiliate content | 80% of MAU | OPTIMISTIC | Depends heavily on UX placement; 60-70% may be more realistic |
| Native ad eCPM | $3.50 | REASONABLE | Travel/lifestyle native ads typically $2-5 on iOS |
| SafetyWing $4.50/mo recurring | VERIFIED | Correct math: 10% of ~$45/mo = $4.50 |
| Blended $30-120/converting user/year | REASONABLE | But "converting user" is the key qualifier -- actual conversion rates will determine real ARPU |

### Revenue Projection Realism by Tier

| MAU Tier | Projected Revenue | Assessment |
|----------|------------------|------------|
| 1,000 MAU | $5-15/mo | REALISTIC -- negligible, correctly stated |
| 10,000 MAU | $360-490/mo | SLIGHTLY OPTIMISTIC -- promoted listings at 5 businesses x $49 assumes sales execution that a solo founder may not have bandwidth for |
| 50,000 MAU | $5,625-6,255/mo | REASONABLE if promoted listings sell; the ad revenue ($2,625) is well-calculated |
| 100,000 MAU | $18,355-19,615/mo | OPTIMISTIC -- assumes 80 businesses paying $99 avg across 15-20 cities, which requires significant B2B sales effort |

### Key Risk Not Addressed in Projections
The projections assume linear scaling of promoted listings revenue, but B2B sales to local businesses is LABOR-INTENSIVE and does not scale automatically. At 50K-100K MAU, the promoted listings line ($2,370-$7,920/mo) requires dedicated sales effort that a solo founder cannot realistically execute while also building product. Either (a) hire for sales, (b) build self-serve, or (c) reduce promoted listings projections by 50%.

1. **Nomad List revenue figure:** Clarify that $5.3M is cumulative, not annual. Do not claim 7.5x YoY growth -- it did not happen.

3. **Nomad List pricing:** Update from $99 to $299.98 (Nomads.com rebrand).
4. **Social features statistics:** Remove or reattribute the "35%/40%/30%" claim. It is vendor marketing, not independent research.
5. **Meetup pricing:** Correct from $55/month to $16.49-$48/month range.

- The phased monetization timeline is sensible
