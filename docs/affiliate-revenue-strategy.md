# x/pat Affiliate Revenue Strategy
## Aych Holdings LLC | March 2026

---

## Executive Summary

x/pat is a free-forever digital nomad social travel app. Revenue comes exclusively from affiliate partnerships — contextual, non-intrusive product recommendations that nomads actually need. This document covers partner selection, integration design, revenue projections, and legal compliance.

**Target**: First affiliate revenue within 60 days of launch. Break-even on operating costs at ~5K MAU.

## 3. INTEGRATION DESIGN

### 3.1 In-App Placement Strategy

Affiliate links must feel like features, not ads. The guiding principle: **recommend what you'd tell a friend**.

#### A. "Nomad Toolkit" Section (Primary Revenue Surface)

A dedicated screen accessible from the profile tab or a toolbar icon. Organized by category:

```
NOMAD TOOLKIT
─────────────────────────
💳 Money & Banking
   Wise — Send money abroad with low fees
   [Coming Soon badge on non-live partners]

🛡️ Insurance
   SafetyWing — Nomad health insurance from $45/mo

📱 Connectivity
   Airalo — eSIM plans for 200+ countries

🔒 Privacy & Security
   NordVPN — Secure your connection anywhere

🏠 Accommodation
   Booking.com — Hotels & apartments
   Hostelworld — Hostels & social stays

📋 Work & Contracts
   Deel — Get paid as a contractor globally
   Remote — Employer of record services
─────────────────────────
```

**Design Notes**:
- Mercury-inspired dark glass aesthetic with subtle gradient cards
- Each card shows: partner logo, one-line value prop, "Learn More" CTA
- Non-live partners show "Coming Soon" (no fake links)
- Small "Affiliate" disclosure label at section header (FTC compliance)

#### B. Contextual Recommendations (Secondary Revenue Surface)

Trigger recommendations based on user behavior:

| Trigger | Recommendation |
|---------|---------------|
| User posts a spot in a new country | "Need an eSIM for [country]?" → Airalo |
| User views spots in expensive city | "Save on stays" → Booking.com/Hostelworld |
| User joins the app (onboarding) | "Essential nomad tools" → SafetyWing, Wise |
| User searches "coworking" or "wifi" | "Secure your connection" → NordVPN |
| User profile says "freelancer" | "Get paid globally" → Deel |

**Implementation**: These appear as subtle, dismissable cards in the feed or spot detail view. Never as popups or interstitials.

#### C. Spot Detail Integration (Tertiary)

When viewing a spot in a specific city/country:
- "Book a stay nearby" → Booking.com deep link to that city
- "Get connected" → Airalo deep link to that country's eSIM

### 3.2 What Works in Similar Apps

Apps like NomadList, Hopper, and Scott's Cheap Flights integrate affiliate content through:
- **Contextual placement**: Recommendations appear where they're relevant, not randomly
- **Value-first framing**: "Save money" or "Essential tool" rather than "Buy this"
- **Minimal friction**: One tap to partner site/app, pre-filled where possible
- **Native feel**: Affiliate cards match app design language exactly

### 3.3 Click Tracking Architecture

**Data to capture per click**:
```
{
  user_id: string,          // anonymized internal ID
  partner_id: string,       // e.g., "safetywing", "wise"
  placement: string,        // e.g., "toolkit", "spot_detail", "feed_card"
  country_context: string,  // country user was viewing (for geo analysis)
  timestamp: ISO8601,
  session_id: string,       // for multi-click attribution
  device_platform: string   // ios/android
}
```

**Storage**: Supabase table `affiliate_clicks` with RLS policies. Aggregate daily for dashboards.

**Attribution flow**:
1. User taps affiliate CTA in x/pat
2. App logs click event to `affiliate_clicks` table
3. App opens partner URL with affiliate tracking parameter (UTM + partner's tracking ID)
4. Partner's cookie/tracking takes over from there
5. Partner reports conversions in their dashboard
6. x/pat cross-references click data with partner conversion reports for optimization

### 3.4 Mobile Conversion Attribution Challenges

| Challenge | Mitigation |
|-----------|-----------|
| **Cookie loss on app-to-browser handoff** | Use partner deep links where available (Airalo SDK, Booking.com deep links). For others, use in-app browser (WebView) to preserve cookies. |
| **Safari ITP blocks third-party cookies** | Most partner networks (Impact, Partnerize, CJ) use server-side tracking that survives ITP. Verify with each partner. |
| **Cross-device tracking** | Not solvable at our scale. Accept that some conversions won't attribute. Focus on partners with long cookie windows (Wise lifetime, SafetyWing 364-day). |
| **App Store restrictions on tracking** | Use ATT (App Tracking Transparency) framework. If user declines tracking, affiliate links still work — we just lose internal analytics on that user. |
| **Delayed conversions** | Partners like Booking.com only pay after guest checkout (could be months later). Build dashboards that account for this lag. |

## 5. LEGAL REQUIREMENTS

### 5.1 FTC Disclosure Requirements

The FTC requires "clear and conspicuous" disclosure whenever you receive compensation for recommending products. This applies to affiliate links in x/pat.

**Requirements**:
- Disclosure must be **near the affiliate content**, not buried in a separate page
- Language must be **plain and understandable**: "We earn a commission if you sign up through these links" or "Affiliate link"
- Must be visible **before** the user clicks — not just on a terms page
- Applies to **all forms**: in-app cards, push notifications mentioning partners, social media posts

**Implementation for x/pat**:
- Nomad Toolkit screen header: "x/pat earns a commission when you use these links. This helps keep the app free forever."
- Individual affiliate cards: Small "Affiliate" badge/label
- Contextual feed cards: "Sponsored recommendation" or "Affiliate" tag
- Terms of Service: Section on affiliate relationships
- About/FAQ: Explanation of how x/pat makes money

**What NOT to do**:
- Hide disclosure behind a tap/scroll
- Use only color or small text that blends with background
- Claim products are "recommended" without disclosing financial relationship
- Use language like "ad" when it's an affiliate link (FTC considers these different)

### 5.2 Apple App Store Guidelines

**Current status (post-May 2025 ruling)**:
- External links to purchase pages are now permitted in US App Store without Apple's interference
- Affiliate links that open Safari/in-app browser to partner sites are allowed
- Apple does NOT take a cut of affiliate commissions (these are not in-app purchases)
- If you ever integrate Airalo's SDK for in-app eSIM purchases, those transactions may be subject to Apple's commission unless structured as a "reader" app or physical goods exception

**Key guidelines to follow**:
- App metadata must accurately reflect the app experience — don't advertise affiliate features as primary app purpose
- Don't use partner brand names/logos in App Store screenshots without permission
- Ensure affiliate functionality doesn't break the core app experience
- AI transparency: If any affiliate recommendations use AI/ML, disclose this

### 5.3 Google Play Store Guidelines

- Similar to Apple: external affiliate links are permitted
- Must not deceive users about the nature of affiliate content
- Ads and monetization policies require clear labeling of commercial content

### 5.4 GDPR Compliance for Affiliate Tracking

**The core rule**: Affiliate tracking cookies/identifiers are NOT "strictly necessary" under GDPR and require explicit user consent before activation.

**Implementation requirements**:
1. **Consent before tracking**: Show consent prompt before logging any affiliate click data to `affiliate_clicks` table for EU users
2. **What requires consent**: The tracking identifiers you attach to outbound links, any analytics on affiliate click behavior, partner cookies set in WebView
3. **What does NOT require consent**: The affiliate link itself (user-initiated action), partner-side tracking (their responsibility)
4. **Consent mechanism**: Integrate with your existing privacy/cookie consent flow. EU users who decline tracking can still use affiliate links — you just don't log the click internally.
5. **Data retention**: Define retention period for affiliate click data (recommend 26 months max, matching partner payout reconciliation needs)
6. **Right to deletion**: Affiliate click data must be deletable upon user request

**Penalties for non-compliance**: Up to 20 million EUR or 4% of global annual revenue.

**Practical approach for launch**: Since x/pat's initial market is likely US-heavy:
- Implement basic consent banner for EU users
- Use server-side tracking where possible (survives cookie restrictions)
- Partner networks (Impact, Partnerize) handle their own GDPR compliance
- Your responsibility is the internal `affiliate_clicks` tracking
