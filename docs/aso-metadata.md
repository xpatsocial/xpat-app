## Description (4000 char max)

```
The travel app built by nomads, for nomads. Completely free. No subscriptions. No paywalls. Ever.

x/pat is a community-curated guide to the best spots around the world -- cafes, coworking spaces, restaurants, bars, parks, and hidden gems that only locals and long-term travelers know about. Every spot is added, rated, and reviewed by real digital nomads and expats who have actually been there.


EXPLORE 1,100+ SPOTS ACROSS 84 CITIES

Open the map and instantly see what is around you -- or search any city you are planning to visit. Every spot includes details that matter to nomads: WiFi reliability, power outlets, noise level, price range, and real reviews from the community.

Whether you are scouting cafes in Chiang Mai, coworking spaces in Lisbon, or rooftop bars in Medellin, x/pat shows you where the nomad community actually goes.


CONNECT WITH TRAVELERS NEARBY

See who else is in your city right now. Follow other nomads, check out their favorite spots, and connect with people who share your lifestyle. No swiping, no matching algorithms -- just a real community of people living and working abroad.

Find travel friends before you land in a new city. See where the community is gathering. Stop traveling alone unless you want to.


SHARE YOUR FAVORITE SPOTS

Found an incredible cafe with fast WiFi and great coffee? A park where you love to work on sunny days? Add it to x/pat so other nomads can find it too. Upload photos, leave detailed reviews, and help build the most comprehensive nomad spot database in the world.

Every spot you add helps another traveler have a better experience in a new city.


NEIGHBORHOOD SAFETY RATINGS

Moving to a new city is exciting, but knowing which neighborhoods are safe matters. x/pat includes community-sourced safety ratings so you can make informed decisions about where to stay, work, and explore -- especially when you are arriving somewhere for the first time.


BUILT FOR THE NOMAD LIFESTYLE

x/pat is not a generic travel app stuffed with tourist traps and sponsored listings. It is built specifically for digital nomads, expats, and remote workers who live abroad and need real, practical information from people who understand the lifestyle.

No promoted spots. No paid placements. Every recommendation comes from the community.


FREE FOREVER

x/pat is and always will be completely free. No subscriptions. No premium tiers. No locked features. Every part of the app is available to every user, always.

We believe the best travel information should be accessible to everyone, not hidden behind a paywall.

Download x/pat and join a global community of nomads helping each other find the best spots on earth.
```
