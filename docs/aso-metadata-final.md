## Full Description (Both Stores)

Discover the world through the eyes of digital nomads who've been there.

x/pat is a free, community-powered app that helps remote workers and digital nomads find the best spots — coworking spaces, cafes with fast WiFi, hidden gems, and local favorites — wherever they roam.

**What makes x/pat different:**

- Community-curated spots with real reviews from fellow nomads
- Live neighborhood pulse ratings so you know what an area is actually like
- City-specific chat rooms to connect with nomads in your area
- Detailed spot info: WiFi speed, power outlets, noise level, pricing
- Interactive dark-mode map with smart filtering
- 100% free — no premium tiers, no paywalls, ever

**Built for nomads, by nomads:**

Whether you're landing in Bangkok, settling into Lisbon, or exploring Mexico City, x/pat gives you instant access to the spots and people that make each city feel like home.

Join thousands of digital nomads sharing their favorite spots and building community around the world.

x/pat is free for life. No subscriptions. No hidden fees. Just the best spots, shared by the best community.
